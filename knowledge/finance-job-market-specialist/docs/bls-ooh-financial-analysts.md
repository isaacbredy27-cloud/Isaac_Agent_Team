# BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook — Financial Analysts

Source: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/business-and-financial/financial-analysts.htm (page dated 2026-08-27; read 2026-11-25)

- "The median annual wage for financial and investment analysts was $102,740 in May 2025." (national)
- "Overall employment of financial analysts is projected to grow 7 percent from 2025 to 2035, much faster than the average for all occupations."
- "Financial analysts typically need a bachelor's degree in finance or a related field such as business. Some employers prefer to hire job candidates who have a master's degree."
- "The Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA) is the main licensing organization for the securities industry. Some financial analyst positions may require licensure."
- "Financial risk specialists, also called financial risk analysts, assess and measure exposure to credit and market risk."
- Important qualities: analytical, communication, computer, decision-making, detail oriented, math.
