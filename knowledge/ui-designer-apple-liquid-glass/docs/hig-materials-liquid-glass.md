# Apple HIG — Materials and Liquid Glass

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials (read 2026-11-25)

- "Liquid Glass is a dynamic material that unifies the design language across Apple platforms, allowing you to present controls and navigation without obscuring underlying content."
- "Liquid Glass forms a distinct functional layer for controls and navigation elements — like tab bars and sidebars — that floats above the content layer."
- **"Don't use Liquid Glass in the content layer."** Use standard materials for content such as app backgrounds. Exception: transient controls (sliders, toggles) take a glass look while active.
- **"Use Liquid Glass effects sparingly."** Limit to the most important functional elements.
- Variants: **regular** "blurs and adjusts the luminosity of background content to maintain legibility" — use for text-heavy components (alerts, sidebars, popovers). **clear** "is highly translucent" — only "for components that appear over visually rich backgrounds" such as photos and video.
- Clear glass legibility: "If the underlying content is bright, consider adding a dark dimming layer of 35% opacity."
- Appearance changes with system settings that reduce transparency or increase contrast.
