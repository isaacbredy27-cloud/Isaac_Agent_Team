# Florida Statutes s. 501.171 — Security of confidential personal information

Source: https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2024/501.171 (2024 Florida Statutes; read 2026-11-25). Summary for engineering; counsel decides legal questions.

## "Personal information" (s. 501.171(1))
a. First name or initial and last name **combined with** any of: SSN; driver license/ID/passport/military ID or similar government number; financial account or card number with required security code/password; medical history, condition, treatment or diagnosis; health insurance policy/subscriber number; biometric data (s. 501.702); **any information regarding an individual's geolocation**.
b. A user name or e-mail address combined with a password or security question and answer that would permit access to an online account.

Implication: resume text alone often falls outside (a), but **account credentials** (b) fall inside, and resumes can contain items from (a). We protect all resume data to the same standard (ADR 0004).

## Duties
- (2) "Each covered entity … shall take reasonable measures to protect and secure data in electronic form containing personal information."
- (3) Notice to the Department of Legal Affairs of a breach affecting **500 or more** individuals in Florida, "no later than 30 days after the determination of the breach or reason to believe a breach occurred."
- (4) Notice to each affected Florida individual "as expeditiously as practicable and without unreasonable delay … but no later than 30 days" after determination, unless an authorized delay or waiver applies.
- (8) Disposal: "take all reasonable measures to dispose … of customer records containing personal information … when the records are no longer to be retained … shredding, erasing, or otherwise modifying the personal information … to make it unreadable or undecipherable."
