# Apple HIG — tab bars and sheets

Sources (read 2026-11-25): https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars · https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets

## Tab bars
- "Use a tab bar to support navigation, not to provide actions."
- "Make sure the tab bar is visible when people navigate to different sections of your app."
- Use the number of tabs needed; "it's generally easier to navigate among fewer tabs."
- "If a section is empty, explain why its content is unavailable."
- "Include tab labels … Use single words whenever possible."
- "Use a badge to indicate that critical information is available."
- The tab bar "floats above content at the bottom of the screen. Its items rest on a Liquid Glass background."

## Sheets
- "Display only one sheet at a time from the main interface." Close the first before showing another.
- "If you provide a Done button, always pair it with a Cancel button" (or Back); avoid all three together.
- Detents: large (full) and medium (~half). "In an iPhone app, consider supporting the medium detent to allow progressive disclosure."
- "Include a grabber in a resizable sheet" — also works with VoiceOver.
- "Support swiping to dismiss a sheet"; confirm if there are unsaved changes.
- "Keep sheet interactions brief and occasional." For long multistep flows consider full-screen modals.
