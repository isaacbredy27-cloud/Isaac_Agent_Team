# Orlando Finance Job Market

Research date: 2026-11-24. Scope: Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford, FL Metropolitan Statistical Area (MSA code 36740). The MSA covers Orange, Osceola, Seminole and Lake counties. That includes Lake Mary, Maitland, Altamonte Springs, Winter Park, Kissimmee and Sanford.

## 1. Key findings

- The Orlando metro employs about **3,110 financial and investment analysts**. Their mean pay is **$98,990** (BLS, May 2025).
- Pay for that group spans **$61,600 (10th percentile) to $140,150 (90th percentile)**. The median is $90,020.
- Live postings cluster in four sectors: **defense and aerospace**, **financial and professional services**, **hospitality and entertainment**, and **healthcare**.
- Defense employers often require **US citizenship and an active Secret clearance**. That is a hard gate. The scoring model must treat it as one.
- Postings almost always ask for **a bachelor's degree, 3–7 years of experience, and advanced Excel**. CPA or a master's degree is usually "preferred", not required.

## 4. Common roles and how they differ

| Role | Typical years | Core work | Signature skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| Financial Analyst | 1–3 | Reporting, variance analysis, budgeting support | Excel, ERP reporting, variance analysis |
| Senior Financial Analyst | 3–7 | Owns models and forecasts; partners with business leaders | Financial modeling, forecasting, presentation |
| FP&A Analyst | 2–6 | Annual plan, rolling forecast, KPI reporting | Driver-based models, Adaptive/Anaplan/Hyperion, BI tools |
| Corporate Finance Analyst | 2–6 | Capital allocation, M&A support, board materials | DCF, valuation, accounting |
| Treasury Analyst | 2–5 | Cash positioning, forecasting, banking, FX/debt | Cash management, TMS (Kyriba), bank portals |
| Credit Analyst | 1–5 | Borrower risk, spreading financials, credit memos | Financial statement analysis, Moody's/S&P tools, ratio analysis |
| Risk Analyst | 2–6 | Market, credit or operational risk measurement | SQL, Python/R, statistics, Basel/CECL |
| Investment Analyst | 1–5 | Security or asset research, portfolio support | Valuation, Bloomberg/FactSet, CFA track |
| Revenue Analyst | 1–5 | Revenue management, pricing, forecasting (big in hospitality) | Forecasting, pricing systems, SQL |
| Finance Manager | 6–10+ | Leads a team or business-unit finance | Leadership, planning cycles, CPA/MBA common |
| Wealth Management Associate | 0–3 | Client service, planning support | FINRA SIE/Series 7/66, CRM, client skills |

Orlando-specific variants:

- **Defense program finance:** earned value management (EVM), Deltek Costpoint, EAC/ETC, DCAA compliance, Secret clearance.
- **Hospitality revenue finance:** revenue management, timeshare/vacation-ownership accounting, pricing.
- **Healthcare finance:** cost accounting, payer mix, reimbursement.

## 5. Qualifications seen in postings

From full descriptions read on 2026-11-24 (Berkadia, AMERICAN SYSTEMS, Reyes Beverage Group, SeaWorld):

- **Education:** bachelor's degree required in every sample. Reyes accepts a high school diploma plus 7–9 years instead. Master's degree or CPA "preferred".
- **Experience:** 3–5 years (Berkadia), 4–6 years (Reyes), 5–7 years (AMERICAN SYSTEMS).
- **Technical:** "exceptionally strong Excel modeling skills", VBA, complex formulas (Berkadia). "Elite Excel skills" (SeaWorld). Deltek Costpoint and EVM (AMERICAN SYSTEMS).
- **Clearance:** "Active Secret Clearance" (AMERICAN SYSTEMS). Lockheed's staff-level posting is titled "SECRET".
- **Work arrangement:** hybrid 3 days in office (AMERICAN SYSTEMS). 25% travel (Reyes). On-site (Berkadia).
- **Soft skills:** communicating complex analysis simply; data visualization; partnering with leaders.

Common tool and skill vocabulary to put in the taxonomy (see `resume-analysis.md`):

- Spreadsheet and modeling: Excel (advanced), pivot tables, INDEX/MATCH, XLOOKUP, Power Query, VBA, three-statement model, DCF, LBO, sensitivity analysis.
- ERP: SAP (S/4HANA, BW), Oracle (EBS, Fusion), Workday Financials, NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics, Deltek Costpoint.
- Planning: Adaptive Planning, Anaplan, Oracle Hyperion/EPM, OneStream, Planful.
- BI and data: Power BI, Tableau, SQL, Python, Alteryx.
- Market data: Bloomberg, FactSet, Capital IQ.
- Domain: GAAP, variance analysis, forecasting, budgeting, month-end close, EVM, CECL, treasury management.

## 7. What predicts hiring success

Evidence strength varies. We rank factors by how often postings treat them as hard requirements, and by how recruiters screen.

1. **Hard gates (binary).** Clearance, citizenship, required licenses (Series 7), and a required degree. Missing one usually ends the application. Source: posting text above.
2. **Required technical skills.** Excel modeling and the named ERP/planning tool. These show up in every "Qualifications" section.
3. **Years of relevant experience.** Each posting states a band. Being far under the band hurts most.
4. **Title and seniority alignment.** Recruiters compare current title to the target title. A "Staff Accountant" applying to "Senior FP&A Analyst" needs translation.
5. **Industry and domain match.** Defense program finance and hospitality revenue management have specific vocabulary. Domain match is a tie-breaker, rarely a gate.
6. **Preferred credentials.** CPA, CFA, MBA. Helpful but rarely decisive for analyst roles. More important for manager roles.

Gaps in evidence:

- We did not find published Orlando-specific studies that link qualifications to hire rates. Rankings above come from posting text, not outcome data.
- The app should log outcomes (applied, interview, offer) with user consent. That data can later calibrate weights. See `match-scoring.md`.

## 8. Implications for the product

- Show BLS local wage bands next to postings without salary.
- Add role families: Analyst, Senior/Staff Analyst, Manager. Use them to pick weight presets.
- Model hard gates separately from weighted scores.
- Seed the employer list with the employers in section 3. Map each to its ATS. See `job-data-sources.md`.
